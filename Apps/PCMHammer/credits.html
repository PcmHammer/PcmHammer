Who To Thank For What

Join us at PcmHacking.net!

Antus - He and Dimented24x7 created the first free tool for reading and writing PCMs (LS1Flash), and he is a major developer here too. More recently he extended PCM Hammer to add support for the P04, P05, P08, P10, P11, P12, E54, and 98/99 Black Box.

NSFW - Tried to write PCM Hammer despite knowing far too little about the hardware side of things, and was rescued by everyone else listed here (Antus says he's too modest). Wrote the framework and supporting classes, and is the primary developer of the logger.

Gampyg28 - Gampy joined the project through the PCMHacking.net forums. First he contributed usability improvements, code tidy-ups, and new features; then he improved the build systems and pipelines. He has been working with Antus on the assembly kernels, which were originally born out of the LS1Flash kernel but have since been developed much further.

rjdrew1986 - For significant amounts of testing, both bench and BDM recovery. For collecting and keeping supported targets and for regression testing when needed.

Crowbarmaster - Recently joined the project and has been working on the Uno UI and the Android builds.

PeteS - He speaks J1850 VPW like a native, and tortures PCMs for sadistic pleasure. His early troubleshooting and testing were invaluable.

Tazzi - His code made it possible for PCM Hammer to support J2534 devices.

Dimented24x7 - For his work on LS1Flash, for sharing his disassembly of a 411 operating system, and for the first free definition of a 411 operating system's calibration data.

Everyone else - Everyone who has tested, reported success or failure or worked with vendors to try and get them to add support for this tool to new interfaces. Everyone else who been part of the PCMHacking.net community, or Facebook group, or anywhere else around the net. It is the community interest that keeps the project going!

Tazzi and PeteS have also built the hardware that we recommend: the OBDX Pro that you see in the list of supported interfaces. It's available from obdxpro.com. The GT, VX, and VT are all compatible with PCM Hammer.
